Paolo Ricaldone

When something truly resonates, it is the stomach that reacts first, before the mind and before words. My works do not begin from concepts to illustrate: they begin when something becomes visceral, before it can be classified.

My drive to create has a relational root. I create the works to make people feel, physically. I cannot hold emotions back: I want those close to me to feel them. I start from my own, but I know the reading belongs to whoever watches, open, theirs.

I do not accompany the works with interpretive keys: I declare the ground they come from, and leave open where they land.

The craft begins only afterwards. I never start from a technique and force an idea into it: the emotion asks for its form, and I build it.

I create the works entirely in CGI, to have no limits on what can be communicated and to abstract without being bound by reality: every frame is a decision, every movement is felt. Today I do not use generative AI, not out of opposition to the tool, but because it does not offer the control and the authorial responsibility I seek.

Each work is a single original, one piece with no editions.

Paolo Ricaldone (Turin, 1968) lives and works in Turin.

He works with silent single-channel video, made entirely in CGI.

The Stage — Acts of a Lucid Silence, a trilogy in three acts begun in 2026, is the current body of work.
