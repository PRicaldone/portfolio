Silence #004

The Silence That Waits

We're in the same house and no one is speaking. I'm in the other room, I hear your steps, the sound of a drawer closing. I could get up and say anything at all, but I don't. I wait for you to come to me.

I tell myself I'm leaving you room, that a word spoken now, only for the sake of breaking the silence, would be worth less than a word of yours that comes on its own.

Then I realize what I'm actually doing. I hold still, but this is not quiet: I count the minutes and I think about who gave in last time. My waiting isn't room I'm leaving you, it's a position I'm defending, and holding it costs me as much as speaking would.

Maybe I'm waiting for you. Or waiting only to be proved right. Pride and patience, from this side of the wall, have exactly the same face.
